## Step 3 – Select Your Best Topic

**Selected Topic:** Cybersecurity Awareness for College Students: How to Recognize Phishing and Protect Personal Information.

I selected this topic because college students frequently use email, social media, educational websites, and other online services. Learning to recognize phishing attempts and protect personal information can help students develop safer online habits.

The topic is relevant to students, beginner-friendly, practical, and suitable for an educational website.

## Step 4 – Prepare the Article Plan

### 1. Proposed Article Title

Cybersecurity Awareness for College Students: How to Recognize Phishing and Protect Personal Information.

### 2. Primary Keyword

Cybersecurity awareness for college students.

### 3. Related Keywords

- Phishing scams for students
- Online safety tips for students
- How to recognize phishing emails
- Password security for students
- Protecting personal information online

### 4. Target Audience

College students, B.E./B.Tech students, engineering students, beginners interested in cybersecurity, and students who regularly use online platforms.

### 5. Purpose of the Article – Why This Content Is Useful

College students regularly use email, social media, educational websites, and online services. They may encounter phishing messages, suspicious links, fake websites, and attempts to steal personal information. This article will explain how to identify common warning signs and follow basic online safety practices. It will help students develop safer digital habits and understand how to respond to suspicious messages.

### 6. Article Structure / Headings

1. Introduction to Cybersecurity Awareness
2. Why Online Safety Matters for College Students
3. What Is Phishing?
4. Common Types of Phishing Scams
   - Email phishing
   - SMS and messaging scams
   - Fake login pages
   - Impersonation messages
5. Warning Signs of a Suspicious Message
6. How to Protect Personal Information Online
7. Creating Strong Passwords and Enabling Multi-Factor Authentication
8. What to Do When You Receive a Suspicious Link
9. What to Do If You Accidentally Share Personal Information
10. Common Online Safety Mistakes to Avoid
11. Practical Example: Identifying a Fake College Account Message
12. Conclusion
